Delete Your Listr Account

You can delete your Listr account directly from the app. This will permanently remove all your data.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Listr app
  2. Tap on your profile icon in the top right corner
  3. Select "Delete account" from the menu
  4. Confirm deletion when prompted

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • All movies and TV series in your lists
  • Your watch status and ratings
  • Your profile information

Need Help?
