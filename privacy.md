# Privacy Policy for Listr

**Last updated: December 5, 2025**

## Information We Collect

**Account Information**
- Email address (for authentication via Firebase Auth)

**User Content**
- Movies and TV series you add to your lists
- Watch status and ratings you assign
- Season tracking data for TV series

## How We Use Your Information

- Authenticate your account
- Store and sync your watch lists across devices
- Provide app functionality

## Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication). Data is processed in accordance with Google's security standards.

## Third-Party Services

- **Firebase** (Google): Authentication and database
- **TMDB API**: Movie/series information (no personal data shared)

## Your Rights

You can:
- Access your data within the app
- Delete your account and all associated data
- Export your data upon request

## Data Retention

We retain your data while your account is active. Upon account deletion, all associated data is permanently removed.

## Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
